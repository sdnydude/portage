More conversation visible. Listings as a horizontal scroll rail - swipeable but secondary to the chat. Chat IS the experience.

Key Design Decisions

Proactive Porter
Porter greets you with overnight insights - watchers, price changes, new messages - not a blank chat.
Expand to Full Screen
↗ button expands chat to the full-screen Porter experience (previous mockup) for longer conversations.
Tab Bar Change
Porter tab replaced by Home. The old dashboard metrics fold into stats row (A) or Porter surfaces them contextually (B).
Voice Always Available
Mic button on home, plus floating mic on other tabs. One assistant, accessible everywhere.
